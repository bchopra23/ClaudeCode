How to read this
You are looking at one manager at a time. The cards below them are the teams that branch further — select one to go down a level. Anyone reporting straight to that manager is listed underneath by name.
What the numbers mean
A number on a card counts everyone in that line, including the person named. Interns are counted separately and never fold into the employee figure.
