How to read this
One tree, top to bottom. Selecting a manager opens their branch in place — everything above and around it stays where it was, so you keep the whole line in view. Select again to close it.
What the numbers mean
A number on a card counts everyone in that line, including the person named. Interns are counted separately and never fold into the employee figure.
